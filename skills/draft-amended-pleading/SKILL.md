# Draft Amended Pleading

## Inputs (must ask)
1) Prior pleading (text)
2) Reason for amendment:
   - court order / opposing counsel meet-and-confer / strategy / new facts
3) Any deficiency findings (order, tentative, meet-and-confer letter)
4) New facts/documents to add
5) Claims/defenses to add/remove
6) Jurisdiction/venue (optional, for overlay)

## Outputs (always)
1) Amended pleading (full text)
2) **Deficiency-cure map**: deficiency → where cured (paragraphs) → supporting facts
3) **Change-log**: added/removed sections (high level)
4) QC report (contradictions + new vulnerabilities)

## Workflow
- Preserve what works; do not rewrite everything unless requested.
- Cure deficiencies with:
  - concrete facts
  - clear actor/date/act structure
  - document references where appropriate
- Avoid adding new vulnerabilities unnecessarily (limitations admissions, notice gaps, etc.).

## QC (required)
- Confirm every deficiency is addressed
- Ensure amended allegations do not conflict with preserved allegations
- Re-run element coverage for new/changed claims
- Remedy alignment check
