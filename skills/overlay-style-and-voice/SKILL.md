# Overlay: Style and Voice

## Purpose
Normalize drafting style across pleadings while preserving substance and legal positions.

## Inputs
- Draft pleading text
- Style pack (preferred) or user preferences:
  - tone (neutral / assertive / restrained)
  - defined term rules
  - headings conventions
  - paragraph length preference
  - citation conventions (if any)

## Output
1) Revised pleading with consistent style
2) Style compliance checklist
3) Any conflicts between style and substance flagged

## Rules
- Do not change admissions/denials, legal positions, or factual meaning.
- Do not "improve" facts. Only improve clarity and structure.
- Keep defined terms consistent and minimal.
